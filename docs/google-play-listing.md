## App access

Public chat, news, and movers are available without signing in. Account creation or sign-in is required for private positions, activity, strategy reports, agent status, and paper orders. Reviewers may create an account in the app.

## Data-safety draft — verify against the final privacy policy

- User-entered chat prompts are sent to the AlpaTrade backend and an AI provider to generate responses.
- Microphone audio is collected only while voice mode is active, sent to the AlpaTrade backend, and relayed to the AI voice provider for transcription and responses.
- Public-market, strategy, portfolio and paper-order data is retrieved from the AlpaTrade backend and Alpaca paper services.
- Language preference and the authentication session are stored locally on the device.
- The app supports email/password account registration. It has no advertising or analytics SDK in the audited `1.0.7` source.
- Confirm hosting logs, retention, encryption-in-transit, deletion handling, and AI-provider retention before completing Play's declarations.
