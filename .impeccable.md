## Design Context

### Users
AlpaTrade serves both investors and active traders who use mobile to quickly understand market catalysts, research a company, and decide what deserves deeper strategy work. It is not intended to be a high-frequency or day-trading terminal.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, and analytical. The interface should create confidence through clear evidence, restrained visual hierarchy, and transparent data quality rather than urgency or hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
Retain the existing parchment-and-forest house style. On mobile, favor a focused research feed with compact event cards and progressive disclosure; retain full data tables for desktop power users. Avoid terminal-like density, alarmist color treatments, and decorative visual noise.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with what changed and why it matters; reveal secondary data on demand.
2. Make source, time, and data quality clear whenever a decision could depend on them.
3. Design touch-first controls with 44px minimum targets and readable, single-column mobile layouts.
4. Preserve desktop depth without forcing mobile users to horizontally scan routine information.
5. Use calm, accessible contrast and motion only to communicate state.
