*   **Downtrending Markets:** "In down-trending markets, not so much" (01:17:36). During these times, "time to sit in cash, do less, and trade with smaller size" (01:17:41-01:17:46). "Breakouts... don't exist in a falling market" (01:18:09-01:18:13).
*   **Post-Correction Entry:** "Best time to trade... is after a pull back in the markets" (28:48-28:51). Look for "stocks that held up the most... during the correction" (29:15-29:29).

### 3. Setup and Entry Rules

*   **Setup Pattern:** "Stair steps" pattern (19:00). A "leg higher" followed by going "sideways or pull back" where "volatility contracts" and the "range gets tighter" before the "next step higher" (19:14-19:42).
*   **Entry Signal:** "Buy it just as it's about to break out into the next step" (19:45-19:55).
*   **Timing Entry (Intraday for daily breakouts):**
    *   The core breakout has to be "obvious on the daily chart" (37:20).
    *   Intraday timing for entry uses "opening range highs" (35:45) based on 1-minute, 5-minute, or 60-minute candles (36:00-36:05).
    *   Wait for the chosen intraday candle (e.g., 1-minute) to "finish and then I buy when it takes out the highs of the day" (36:19-36:23).
    *   Avoid anticipating breakouts; "I buy breakouts, I don't anticipate them... I buy them as they break out" (38:04-38:10).
*   **Fundamental Angle (Conviction Builder):** "Combine it with fundamentals" (17:19). Identify the "fuel" behind the move (31:24). Look for "fastest growing stocks" (31:52) (earnings/revenue growth) or "story stocks, concept stocks" with strong narratives (32:02, 32:23-32:48).

### 4. Stop Loss

*   **Initial Stop:** "lows of the day" (36:25, 41:46) of the breakout candle.
*   **Absolute Stop:** "I have one absolute stop where I like okay, I get out no matter what" (42:35-42:38).

### 5. Exits

*   **Partial Profits:** "I always sell some into that first burst" (33:34-33:37), usually "20% or 25%" (33:40) to "lock in a little bit of profits" (33:45). This is for the "most predictable part of the move" (33:28-33:32).
*   **Trailing Stop (for remaining position):** Uses "the 10 and 20 day moving averages" (20:17, 42:10) depending on stock volatility (faster stocks use 10-day MA, slower use 20-day MA, 41:56-42:02).
*   **Trailing Stop Trigger:** "I only wait for the close... if it's kind of obvious it's gonna close below that moving average, that's when I close the rest of my position" (42:47-42:58). Ignores intraday violations of the moving average (42:59-43:29).
*   **Avoiding Catalysts:** "If it's an earnings... I really don't like to hold my positions into earnings or into any type of catalyst, I just sell it" (41:00-41:20). "I just want to control my risk as much as possible. I don't really like to gamble over over earnings" (42:25-42:31).

### 6. Position Sizing and Risk Per Trade

*   **Entry Sizing:** "Buy everything at once... very aggressively" (34:37-34:40).
*   **Scaling Up:** "If I double my account, my risk and my size will double also" (01:00:08). "Always bringing my... risk and my size up" (01:00:20-01:00:23). This is done in "percentage terms" (01:00:26-01:00:29, 01:09:09-01:09:12).

*   **Diversification:** "You can't have everything in two or three stocks. You got to diversify a bit more" (01:02:41-01:02:44).
*   **Number of Positions:** Ideally "between 15-20" (01:12:25-01:12:30). Has had "30 positions sometimes" (01:09:53-01:09:54). These positions are rolled over, where "partial profits" from one are reinvested into "new positions, new breakouts" (01:09:40-01:09:47). A new entry into an existing stock is treated as a "new trade with the same rules" (01:00:46-01:00:50).
*   **Win Rate/Payoff:** Low win rate ("35%" last year, "25%" the year before, 01:29:24-01:29:33) but "all about... big winners" (01:29:58). "few big winners that were most of my money comes from" (01:30:17-01:30:20). "Home run trader" (01:30:51).

### 7. Timeframe and Holding Period

*   **Primary Timeframe:** Daily charts (18:25). Intraday charts (1m, 5m, 60m) are used for precise entry timing (36:00-36:05).
*   **Holding Period:** Typically "several weeks and sometimes even several months" (21:44-21:47). Can be "three to five days" (20:03) if selling into initial strength.
